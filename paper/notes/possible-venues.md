# Possible venues

## Primary candidate

**Language Sciences** — natural fit given sister-paper precedent (`What_do_we_mean_by_language/` LingBuzz 009947 was targeted there) and the metaphysical-linguistic shape of the argument. The journal welcomes work that enlarges the view of language and linguistic categories, debates on metatheoretical and epistemological issues, and scrutiny of how linguistic objects are constructed.

## Alternatives by argument shape

| Venue | Conditional shift |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Theoretical Linguistics** | If the metaphysical framing becomes the centre and the case studies become illustrations. |
| **Linguistic Typology** | If §6 (typological transparency) grows to dominate. Probably not the right fit unless §6 expands substantially. |
| **Cognitive Linguistics** | If §3 (constructional transparency) becomes the centre and the article's audience shifts toward CxG specialists. |
| **Studies in Language** | If the paper picks up a strong cross-linguistic comparative arc. |
| **Journal of Linguistics** | If the argument shape becomes more conventionally syntactic; less likely fit. |
| **Mind & Language** | Only if §7 (metaphysics) becomes much more central and the paper shifts toward philosophy of linguistics. |

## Co-authored shape

If the project becomes a Reynolds-Kim collaboration, **Korean Linguistics** or **Studies in Generative Grammar** might enter the candidate list, with the Korean morphosyntactic-transparency cases doing more substantive work. *Language Sciences* would still be a candidate; the comparative-linguistic ambit fits.

## Recommendation

Target *Language Sciences* unless the article's shape pulls it elsewhere during drafting. The decision can stay open until after a complete draft.

## Notes

- House style and length differ across venues. Locking the venue too early creates rework.
