# Abstract, Introduction, and Conclusion Update Plan
## Goal
Revise the abstract, introduction, and conclusion so they reflect the newly added §6 material without changing the paper's center of gravity.
## Load-bearing assumptions
1. The new topology material should strengthen the umbrella schema, not turn the paper into a survey of exotic transparency phenomena.
  
2. Aleut is the only new case that should be named as a qualified positive case close to the original head-marking question.
  
3. Kayardild/Lardil and Chamorro should function as topology pressure tests: inward access and path-distributed access, not new worked profiles.
  
4. Algonquian/Kutenai obviation should remain a boundary/negative-control case because it is feature relay rather than direct access.
  
5. Korean should remain deferred, but it should no longer carry all the weight as the next cross-linguistic test.
  
## Proposed edits
### Abstract
Replace the current Spanish/Tsez/Korean sentence with a broader typology sentence:

- English QNs and TFRs remain the primary syntactic results.
  
- Spanish and Tsez show outward endpoint access outside English.
  
- Aleut, Kayardild/Lardil, Chamorro, and Algonquian/Kutenai show that the schema also distinguishes null-source, inward, path-distributed, and relay-like topologies.
  
- Phrase these as pressure tests on the schema, not as fully developed projectibility profiles.
  
### Introduction
Add a compact paragraph after the TFR paragraph, before the field-relativity paragraph:

- Spanish and Tsez extend the outward endpoint pattern.
  
- Aleut shows that $P$ can be recoverable rather than overt, with the qualification that Merchant analyzes the missing possessor as syntactically present.
  
- Kayardild/Lardil and Chamorro show that accessibility can run inward or along a path.
  
- Algonquian/Kutenai obviation marks the boundary where a feature is relayed onto a head rather than accessed directly.
  

Then update the scope paragraph:

- Keep extraction, islands, barriers, and phases outside the worked evidence.
  
- Add that Chamorro appears only as a topology comparison in §6.
  

Update the roadmap:

- Section 4 should now be described as applying Haspelmath and introducing the topology map, not just cross-linguistic ambitions.
  
### Conclusion
Replace the current one-sentence topology paragraph with a fuller closing synthesis:

- The additions change the topology of the schema: not just "internal property escaping a mediator," but outward, inward, path-distributed, null-source, and relay-boundary configurations.
  
- State that Aleut is a qualified positive for null-source subpart access.
  
- State that Kayardild/Lardil and Chamorro widen the typology but are not promoted to core worked profiles.
  
- State that obviation shows why transformed or relayed features should not be counted as direct transparency.
  
## Falsification / restraint conditions
The revision should fail if it:

- makes Aleut sound like an unqualified overt NP-internal agreement case;
  
- makes Kayardild/Lardil or Chamorro sound like fully worked projectibility profiles;
  
- weakens the paper's main result about English QNs and TFRs;
  
- contradicts the "extraction acknowledged but not developed" scope boundary;
  
- adds new citations or claims not already grounded in §6 and the bibliography.
