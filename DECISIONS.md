## 2026-05-01 — Reframing: projectibility-first

- **2026-05-01** — **Master question reframed.** The paper's central question is no longer "which transparency senses are local kinds?" but "when do transparency attributions support induction, and what stabilises that induction?" The umbrella schema is a tool for locating *projectibility profiles*: observable bases licensing non-trivial projection to targets, stabilised by identifiable sources. Different profiles are stabilised differently (lexical convention, constructional inheritance, usage-based entrenchment, processing architecture, formal-semantic environment). Reason: three review-board rounds converged on the diagnosis that the paper buries projectibility under classification work; Brett's email to MA Khalidi (about the HPC book; cf. 2026-05-01 conversation) flagged the same instinct independently. Codex's third pass confirmed the reframing improves the paper. Sister projects (*What do we mean by language*, *Field-relative HPC categories*) settled on Slater + maintenance-as-umbrella; this paper now treats SPC and Khalidi as *diagnostic tools* rather than master ontologies. Programme coherence is preserved at the diagnostic-toolkit level, not the master-ontology level.
- **2026-05-01** — **Vocabulary shift.** *Local-kind candidate* → *projectibility profile* across §§1, 2.4, 4.4, 5.5, 6, 7, 8, abstract. *Local kind* reserved for cases where the profile is especially stable and broadly projectible (kindhood as downstream verdict on diagnostics). Reason: the projectibility-first reframing makes profile-as-unit-of-analysis primary; *local kind* becomes the verdict on a profile, not the unit itself.
- **2026-05-01** — **"Stabilising structures" preferred over "causal networks" as headline frame.** Reason: Codex flagged that "linguistic categories are projectible positions in causal networks" overreaches if "causal" is read narrowly as Khalidi-style directed hierarchy. Linguistic stabilisation is heterogeneous (convention, inheritance, usage, processing, formal structure). "Stabilising structures" with causal networks as one high-grade subtype is the more accurate frame; Khalidi's apparatus is reserved for cases that earn it.
- **2026-05-01** — **§7 retitled "Projectibility profiles."** Reason: matches the unit of analysis; replaces "Metaphysical framing" which signalled the old kindhood-as-master question.
