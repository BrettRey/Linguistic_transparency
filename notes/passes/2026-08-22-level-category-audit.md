# Level and category audit: umbrella paper

Date: 2026-08-22
Verdict: pass after two bounded repairs

The manuscript successfully distinguishes the relation from its diagnostics, the comparative concept from language-particular descriptive categories, the relational verdict from the profile verdict, and the evidential profile from any proposed ground. It also avoids treating cross-linguistic recurrence as evidence for one universal grammatical category.

Two local failures remain:

1. Appendix B called Chamorro “positive as a topology” although the paper's own four-gate protocol makes it indeterminate without a same-family concealing counterpart. Lardil/Kayardild had the same missing status label. Both table cells have been corrected to “candidate topology; application indeterminate”.
2. The projectibility definition fuses evidential warrant with an identified stabilizer. That is a level error between a warranted projection and a proposed world-side ground; it is handled in the projectibility repair.

One rival-analysis sentence also needs a reader-pass repair: Wilder's parenthetical analysis may dissolve the mediated relation rather than merely relocate its mediator. That wording is reserved for the reviewable prose checkpoint.
