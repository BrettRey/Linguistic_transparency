# Charitable-engagement and source-positioning audit: umbrella paper

Date: 2026-08-22
Verdict: pass after three wording repairs

Primary-source rereading confirms the manuscript's central positioning:

- Haspelmath distinguishes language-particular descriptive categories from analyst-created comparative concepts.
- Leufkens defines transparency through one-to-one form--meaning relations and treats agreement under redundancy.
- Slater drops mechanisms as a necessary or sufficient condition and assesses stable clusters relative to inferential purposes.
- Newmeyer's negative-particle/negative-auxiliary example is accurately reported from p. 136: the two formal realizations pattern differently in word-order typology.
- Kim's current manuscript and book support the verb classes, the TFR feature distinctions, the parent/subtype proposal, and the summaries of Wilder, van Riemsdijk, and Grosu.

Three passages need refinement:

1. Boyd should be described as offering a mechanism-based account of property co-occurrence, not merely a “maintenance-based account of stability”.
2. Khalidi's view concerns ordered causal dependence among properties, not “ordered causal stabilizers”.
3. Wilder's parenthetical analysis may dissolve the relation as framed, rather than locating the same mediator elsewhere.

The paper otherwise represents rivals as live analyses, limits disagreement to what the evidence bears on, and distinguishes an untested explanation from an established result.
