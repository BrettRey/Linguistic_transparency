# Coherence and reader-pass edits for both papers

These are the remaining substantive prose changes after the conceptual and evidence audits. They do not change counts, classifications, examples, citations, or the paper architecture. The umbrella changes separate evidential warrant from a proposed world-side stabilizer; the companion changes separate the archived distribution from Kim's analysis of it.
