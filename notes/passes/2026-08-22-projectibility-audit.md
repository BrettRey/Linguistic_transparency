# Projectibility audit: umbrella paper

Date: 2026-08-22
Scope: `main.tex` and all included sections
Verdict: pass after the specified revisions

## Resolution

The final text separates evidential warrant from a proposed world-side stabilizer, specifies the QN population, timescale, tolerance, and revision conditions, and uses source-faithful descriptions of Boyd and Khalidi. The red and yellow findings below record the state that motivated those repairs; none remains unresolved.

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Declaration | YELLOW | The QN profile names an independently observable input and multiple targets, but the operative population, synchronic timescale, and tolerated exceptions are not yet stated together. |
| Inquiry/use, warrant, revision | YELLOW | Description and candidate discovery are distinct uses; the `piles` pilot tests one prospective candidate and is properly demoted after the feasibility failure. The revision rule is spread across the section rather than declared once. |

## Required repair

- Define a projectibility profile by its observable base, target, population, and evidential warrant. Treat an identified stabilizer as a stronger, separate world-side explanation.
- In §2.4, state that the current population is contemporary English QN uses in the specified frames, that sporadic performance noise is tolerated but systematic counter-patterning is not, and that systematic failure revises membership or the proposed target.
- Replace the summaries of Boyd and Khalidi with source-faithful descriptions: Boyd offers a mechanism-based account of property co-occurrence; Khalidi concerns ordered causal dependence among properties.

## Bottom line

**Is projectibility structural or decorative?** Structural. It supplies the paper's second-stage test after applicability and changes what can be inferred from a positive relational verdict.

**Does the draft overclaim world-side commitment?** It did at four framing points. The final text now treats the QN evidence as support for a candidate stable profile and its conventional ground as proposed rather than demonstrated.

**What would change structurally?** No architectural rewrite. Revise the projectibility definition and add one compact declaration-and-revision paragraph to §2.4; the rest of the argument can remain in place.
