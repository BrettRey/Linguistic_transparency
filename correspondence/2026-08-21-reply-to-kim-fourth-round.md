The mechanical items first. The abstract is one paragraph, about 165 words. Line numbers now come every five lines (the modulo option you named). If you'd still rather park the whole subsection and restore it only if a reviewer asks, it's an easy cut, so say the word.

I kept §5.1 intact and collapsed the old §§5.2–5.4 into one short subsection (the evidential grades, the two proposed stabilizers, the three levels). The section is now about two pages, and the introduction still tells readers who only want the grammar to skip it.

Your three confirmations are all implemented as we discussed. The TFR verdict reads analysis-relative in §2.5, Table 4, and the conclusion: positive under your constructional analysis, indeterminate analysis-neutrally. And your collectives sentence is in §2.2; while placing it I found a third leftover in §2.3 that still grouped collectives with QNs as mediated accessibility, now fixed to match.
